Tarifly is your personal digital cookbook, powered by AI. Save your own recipes, organize them into folders, generate new ones in seconds and never wonder what to cook again.

WHY TARIFLY?
• Add Your Own Recipes: Keep family favorites and your own creations in one place, with ingredients and step-by-step instructions.
• Organize with Folders: Group recipes into folders like Desserts, Soups, Main Dishes or Breakfast and find them in seconds.
• AI Recipe Generator: Type a dish name and Tarifly's AI chef creates a detailed recipe with ingredients, steps and measurements.
• What Should I Cook Today?: Out of ideas? Get instant meal suggestions and discover something new for breakfast, lunch or dinner.
• Share Recipes: Send your favorite recipes to friends and family with a single tap.
• Favorites: Bookmark the dishes you love for quick access anytime.
• 6 Languages: Fully localized in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese and Turkish.
• Dark & Light Mode: A clean, modern design that's easy on the eyes in the kitchen.
